## Intent

Launch Reel for the ActivateMe Fest "What should my kid try?" quiz (activateme-quiz.pages.dev).
Concept #1 "Twelve answers": an Apple-keynote-style reveal — one word at a time on the beat
("Football." "Swimming." "Chess."), each time with Acti in that sport, landing on
"Which one is your kid's?", then the real quiz gliding in on a phone.
User's words: "so aesthetically pleasing like an apple video but still with our brand colors".

## Assets

- ../../public/assets/acti-card/*.webp — HD Acti cut-outs: 12 sport poses + reactions (wave, point, think...).
- ../../public/assets/logo.webp — ActivateMe heart logo (transparent).
- ../../public/assets/fonts/baloo2-latin.woff2 — Baloo 2, brand font.
- The quiz itself (this repo, run locally) — real screens for the phone shot.

## Customizations

- Five sports get a full beat each (Football, Swimming, Chess, Cycling, Creativity); the other seven
  flash as a fast flicker of Acti poses, so "all twelve" are seen without crowding.
- Every cut on the music's beat; instrumental music bed, no voiceover (text carries the story).
- End card: "What should my kid try?" · "Take the quiz · link in bio" · "16–17 Jan 2027 · Dubai Silicon Oasis"
  · logo · @activatemefest.

## Notes

- Brand palette: purple #4a01e0, magenta #b01ab8, pink #f83840, orange #fc8700, ink #1b0b46.
  Signature look is the purple → magenta → pink → orange gradient; used as light/accent on a deep ink stage.
- Apple-like restraint: large crisp type, generous space, springy but smooth motion, one idea per beat.
- Reel safe zones: keep text out of the bottom ~20% and right edge (Instagram UI).
- Tone: playful, friendly, never salesy.
